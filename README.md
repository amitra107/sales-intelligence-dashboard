\## Key Insights from the Analysis



The analysis of the 10,000-record synthetic dataset produced the following observations:



\- Total sales were approximately ₹91.99 lakh with total profit of ₹28.90 lakh.

\- Overall calculated profit margin was 31.42%.

\- Regional performance was relatively balanced, with West recording the highest sales at approximately ₹23.36 lakh.

\- Electronics was the largest category, generating approximately ₹62.15 lakh in sales.

\- Laptop was the highest-selling product, generating approximately ₹29.90 lakh in sales and ₹9.41 lakh in profit.

\- August recorded the highest monthly sales at approximately ₹8.31 lakh.

\- Sales were generally stronger during the later months of the year compared with January.
